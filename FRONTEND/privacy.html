Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Policy FAQs Contact & Support

Welcome to Snowwflix. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting the personal information you share while using our platform. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices you have regarding your personal data.

Information We Collect

When you create a Snowwflix account, we may collect information such as your name, email address, profile picture, and account preferences. We also collect information related to your activity on the platform, including your watch history, saved favorites, search history, and viewing preferences. This information helps us personalize your experience and recommend content that matches your interests.

Snowwflix may also collect technical information such as your device type, browser version, operating system, IP address, language settings, and general usage statistics. These details help us improve performance, diagnose technical issues, enhance security, and provide a better overall user experience.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate and maintain Snowwflix, provide customer support, improve existing features, develop new services, send important account notifications, and protect the platform from fraud or unauthorized access. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

How We Protect Your Information

Your information is stored using appropriate security measures designed to protect it against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. While we work hard to keep your data secure, no online service can guarantee absolute security, and users should also take reasonable steps to protect their account credentials.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Snowwflix may use cookies or similar technologies to remember your preferences, keep you signed in, analyze platform usage, and improve site functionality. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings, although doing so may affect certain features of the platform.

Your Choices and Rights

You have control over your personal information. You may update your account details, change your profile settings, or request the deletion of your account at any time. If you choose to delete your account, we will make reasonable efforts to remove your personal information in accordance with applicable laws and operational requirements.

Changes to This Policy

Snowwflix may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our services evolve or legal requirements change. Any significant updates will be communicated through the platform, and the revised policy will become effective once published.

Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact the Snowwflix Support Team using the details in the Contact & Support section below.

By creating an account or continuing to use Snowwflix, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to the collection and use of your information as described above.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to accept the Privacy Policy to use Snowwflix?

We ask you to read and accept this Privacy Policy so you understand how we collect and use your information. Accepting records your consent. You can open the app and review this page anytime from Settings → Privacy & Legal.

What happens if I decline?

If you decline, your choice is recorded on this page. You can still return to the Snowwflix app. Some personalized features that rely on cookies and account data work best when you accept the policy. You can change your mind and accept later.

Do you sell my personal data?

No. Snowwflix does not sell your personal information to third parties.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes. You may request account deletion at any time from your account settings or by contacting support. We will make reasonable efforts to remove your personal information in accordance with applicable laws.

How do I manage cookies?

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect sign-in, preferences, and other features of the platform.

How can I contact support?
